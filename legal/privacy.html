The Short Version

The Skyrim Character Combat Optimizer (the “Site”) is a free personal project operated by C. J. Wiebe, who is the party responsible for the personal information described here. This policy explains what the Site collects, why, where it is stored, and what you can do about it.

  • You can browse the item tables, the home page and the references list without an account, and without giving us anything.
  • An account stores your name, email, optional date of birth, and the characters you build.
  • There is no advertising, no analytics, no tracking pixels, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.
  • Your data lives in Google Firebase. You can delete all of it yourself, at any time, from your account page.

This policy is written with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) in mind. Because the Site is reachable from anywhere, sections 5 and 10 also cover visitors in the United Kingdom and the European Economic Area.

Information you create while using the Site

Everything below is stored under your own account, is visible only to you, and exists solely so your work is still there when you come back:

  • Characters — name, race, level, skills, attribute choices and selected perks;
  • Inventory — which items each character carries, their quantities and upgrade levels, and what is equipped;
  • Spellbook — which spells each character has learned.

Information collected automatically

The Site itself runs no analytics and sets no tracking cookies. However, the companies that host and serve it keep their own operational records, which typically include your IP address, browser type and the time of a request:

  • GitHub Pages serves the pages, images and data files;
  • Google Firebase handles sign-in and database requests, and keeps sign-in security records such as the date of your last login;
  • jsDelivr serves the Bootstrap stylesheet and script.

These records are held by those companies under their own privacy policies, linked in section 6. We do not receive, request or have access to them.

What We Do Not Collect

Stating this plainly, because the absence is the point:

  • No payment information. The Site is free and has no payment feature of any kind.
  • No analytics. No Google Analytics, no tracking pixels, no heatmaps, no session recording.
  • No advertising. No ad networks, no advertising identifiers, no behavioural profiles.
  • No address, phone number or government identifier. None of these is asked for or stored.
  • No location data. The Site does not request geolocation.
  • No contacts, files or device data beyond what your browser sends with an ordinary web request.
  • No sensitive categories. We do not ask about health, beliefs, or any similar category, and you should not enter them into a character name or other free-text field.

How Your Information Is Used

Your information is used only to run the Site:

  • To create your account and sign you in;
  • To save your characters, inventories and spellbooks, and show them back to you;
  • To run the combat calculations on the character data you enter;
  • To let you update your profile, email address or password;
  • To send transactional email that Firebase generates on your request — a password reset, or a confirmation of an email change;
  • To keep the Site secure and to enforce our Terms and Conditions;
  • To respond when you contact us.

Your information is never used for advertising, sold or rented, used to build a profile of you, or shared with anyone for their own purposes. We do not send marketing email — there is no mailing list.

No automated decision-making with legal or similarly significant effects is carried out. The Site's calculations operate on fictional game statistics, not on you.

Consent

Under PIPEDA we rely on your consent. You give it by creating an account and entering information, having had this policy available to you beforehand. Because the information involved is limited and the purposes are the obvious ones, that consent is implied by your use of the Site — but it is yours to withdraw.

You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account, which removes the information described in section 2. The practical consequence is simply that the Site can no longer sign you in or keep your characters.

For visitors in the UK or EEA, the lawful bases are performance of a contract (providing the account you asked for) and legitimate interests (keeping the Site secure and working).

Cookies and Browser Storage

The Site sets no advertising or analytics cookies, and there is nothing to opt out of on that front.

Firebase Authentication does store a sign-in token in your browser's local storage and IndexedDB. That is what keeps you signed in between page loads and visits; it is strictly necessary for the Site to work and cannot be turned off while still having an account. It stays on your device, and is not readable by other websites.

Signing out, or clearing your browser's site data, removes it. Blocking storage for this Site entirely will prevent sign-in from working — the reference pages will still be readable.

Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. It is disclosed only in these circumstances:

  • To the service providers in section 6, and only as needed for them to host the Site and store your data on our behalf;
  • Where the law requires it — a valid court order, subpoena or other lawful demand;
  • To protect rights and safety — where disclosure is necessary to investigate a suspected breach of our Terms, fraud, or a threat to the security of the Site or its users;
  • With your consent, for any other purpose you agree to.

Other users cannot see your account or your characters. The security rules published to Firestore permit a signed-in user to read and write only the documents beneath their own account, and nothing else.

How Long It Is Kept

Your information is kept for as long as your account exists. There is no automatic expiry, and nothing is archived after deletion.

Deleting your account

Go to My Account → Delete Account. The deletion runs in this order, and all of it is permanent:

  1. Every character's inventory and spellbook;
  2. Every character;
  3. Your profile document — name, email and date of birth;
  4. Your Firebase Authentication user, including your stored password hash.

You can also delete a single character, or clear an inventory, without closing your account.

After deletion the information is gone from the live database and is not recoverable — by you or by us. Residual copies may persist briefly in Google's routine infrastructure backups before being overwritten on their own schedule, and operational logs held by the providers in section 6 age out under their retention policies.

Your Rights

Most of these you can exercise yourself, immediately, without asking us:

  • Access — everything we hold about you is displayed on your account and character pages. For a copy in another form, write to us.
  • Correction — update your name, date of birth or email from My Account → Update Account.
  • Deletion — delete individual characters, or the whole account, as described in section 9.
  • Withdraw consent — by deleting your account.
  • Complain — if we have not handled your information properly, contact us first so we can put it right. You may also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

If you are in the UK or EEA

You additionally have the rights to data portability, to restrict or object to processing, and to lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority. Write to the address in section 13 to exercise any of them.

We will respond to any request within 30 days. We may need to confirm you control the account's email address before acting on it.

Security

Reasonable safeguards are in place for a project of this size:

  • All traffic is served over HTTPS;
  • Passwords are handled and hashed by Firebase Authentication. They never reach this Site's code, and neither we nor anyone else can read them;
  • Firestore security rules enforce, on Google's servers, that a signed-in user may reach only their own data — the browser cannot talk its way around them;
  • Changing your email or password requires re-entering your current password first;
  • The Site holds no server of its own and no credentials that could be stolen from one.
No system is perfectly secure. This is a personal project maintained by one person, not a service with a security team behind it. Please use a password unique to this Site, and do not store anything sensitive in a character name or other free-text field.

If you discover a vulnerability, please report it to the address in section 13 rather than disclosing it publicly.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us at the address below and we will delete the account and its data.
