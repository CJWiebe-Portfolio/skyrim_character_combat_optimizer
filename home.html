The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim

Character Combat Optimizer

Creating an account allows you to save your characters and their builds. Pick a race, input your current characters skills and perks, then fill in their inventory with weapons, armour and spells. The site will summarize the inventory which allows you to see how your choices affect your level, damage, armour rating and what you can actually carry.

Created by C. J. Wiebe

Features of the website:

  1. 1

    Creating an account:

    Characters are saved to your account, allowing you to access them from any machine. Limited usage for those not signed in, but browsing the item list and the spell list needs no account at all.

  2. 2

    Creating a character:

    Give your character a name and select one of the ten Skyrim races. Each race determines which starting skills have higher values, and your beginning level in Health, Magicka and Stamina.

  3. 3

    Customizing the character:

    Raise any of the eighteen skills, spend perk points in the skills, and put each level-up into Health, Magicka or Stamina. The level is derived from the skills you have raised.

  4. 4

    Equipping new gear and spells:

    Add weapons, armour, and spells to each character. Summarizing the best weapon damage, armour rating, damage reduction and carry weight all move as you edit.
