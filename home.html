The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim

Character Combat Optimizer

Plan a character on paper before you spend the perk point. Pick a race, raise skills, take perks and fill the inventory — the sheet works out your level, your damage, your armour rating and what you can actually carry.

Created by C. J. Wiebe

How to use it

  1. 1

    Make an account

    Characters are saved against your account, so the same roster is there whichever machine you open it on. Browsing the item list needs no account at all.

  2. 2

    Add a character

    Give them a name and one of the ten races. The race decides which skills you start above the floor, and your opening Health, Magicka and Stamina.

  3. 3

    Build the sheet

    Raise any of the eighteen skills, spend perk points in the One-Handed, Two-Handed, Heavy Armor and Light Armor trees, and put each level-up into Health, Magicka or Stamina. The level is derived from the skills you have raised.

  4. 4

    Fit the gear

    Add weapons and armour to the character's inventory on that same page. Best weapon damage, armour rating, damage reduction and carry weight all move as you edit — no flipping between pages to see the effect.
